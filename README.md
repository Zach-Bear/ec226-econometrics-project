# ec226-econometrics-project
Submission to EC226 Project "Does Homeownership Increase Life Satisfaction?". Empirical analysis of the data from a UK Longitudinal Household Study using R.

## Executive Summary
Taking UKLHS Data on 40,000 households over a number of years we set out to answer if home ownership is still a metric worth striving for in UK Government Policy and Economic Research. Tackling this question we compared the reported life satisfaction of households in different tenure brackets, measuring the relative impact of ownership and outright ownership on households across different regions and age groups. While we expected the added stability of home ownership to raise happiness across the board, as it turned out this was only the case for fully financed ownership (those with a mortgage reporting similar satisfaction to renters), and mattered far more to older generations. Surprisingly we found that the COVID pandemic didn't create a divergence between satisfaction for those quarantining in their own home versus in rented accomodation. There were lots of other curious results and non-results we had to leave out of our final finding just like this, you can message me if you care about it for possible future research.

## Methodology & Tools
- **Data & Techniques:** OLS, Fixed Effects, Instrumental Variable: Regional House Price Fluctuations
- **Programming:** R
- **Documentation:** Structured and typeset in LaTeX
